HOLLOWMERE PARISH. CENSUS OF 30 MARCH 1851.
Enumerator: Thomas Wick, parish clerk.
Each entry gives the dwelling, the head of household in capitals, age and occupation, then the other persons present.

Household 1. Mill House. REEVE, Josiah, 47, miller. Wife Hannah, 44. Children: Samuel, 19; Mary, 15. Persons: 4.
Household 2. Church Cottage. DUNMORE, Edmund, 52, clergyman. Wife Agnes, 49. Persons: 2.
Household 3. Long Croft Farm. ASHBY, William, 40, farmer. Wife Ruth, 38. Children: John, 12; Ellen, 9; Peter, 6. Persons: 5.
Household 4. Carrow's Yard. CARROW, Matthew, 35, blacksmith. Wife Jane, 33. Children: Thomas, 8. Persons: 3.
Household 5. Bridge End. FENWICK, Alice, 61, widow, laundress. Persons: 1.
Household 6. Hale's Barn. HALE, Robert, 29, carter. Wife Susan, 27. Children: Ann, 2. Persons: 3.
Household 7. The Green. GRICE, Daniel, 44, publican at the Plough. Wife Martha, 41. Children: George, 14; Lucy, 11. Persons: 4.
Household 8. Lumley Row. LUMLEY, Peter, 58, cordwainer. Persons: 1.
Household 9. Upper Field. PARGETER, Henry, 36, farmer. Wife Sarah, 34. Children: Henry, 10; Jane, 7; Mark, 4; Ruth, 1. Persons: 6.
Household 10. Tolley's Cottage. TOLLEY, Joseph, 50, labourer. Wife Mary, 48. Children: James, 17. Persons: 3.
Household 11. Schoolhouse. BRAMWELL, Eliza, 39, schoolmistress. Persons: 1.
Household 12. Wick's End. WICK, Thomas, 45, parish clerk. Wife Charlotte, 43. Children: Thomas, 13; Rose, 10. Persons: 4.

Enumerator's note: no house in the parish was found empty. Netherby, across the river, is a separate parish and is not counted here.
