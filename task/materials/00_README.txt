HOLLOWMERE PARISH RECORDS, 1850 TO 1855
A small archive assembled for the civ demo. Everything here is fictional.

Files
  census_1851.txt          the household census of 30 March 1851
  ledger_1852.txt          the mill's receipts for the year 1852
  parish_log_1850_1855.txt the clerk's dated log of parish events
  letter_1853_ashby.txt    Ruth Ashby to her sister, after the mill fire
  letter_1854_reeve.txt    Samuel Reeve to the insurers
  letter_1855_dunmore.txt  the Reverend Dunmore to the Archdeacon
  inventory_1854.txt       what was lost in the fire, with valuations
  tithe_notes_1850.txt     field names, occupiers and acreage

Conventions
  Dates in the log are written year-month-day.
  Money is in pounds and shillings; twenty shillings make a pound.
  Surnames in the census are written in capitals.
