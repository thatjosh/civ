Church Cottage, Hollowmere
30 September 1855

To the Venerable the Archdeacon

My dear Archdeacon,

I am glad to report that the mill at Hollowmere reopened yesterday, at Michaelmas, two years all but a week after it burned. Samuel Reeve is miller in his late father's place, and the first grist was brought by William Ashby, as his wife had promised it would be.

You asked after the cost. The Society paid eighty pounds against an inventory of ninety-six, and the sixteen pounds short was made up by subscription in the parish, Daniel Grice at the Plough giving the largest part. The masons were from Netherby and were engaged in June of last year.

With my respectful good wishes,
Edmund Dunmore
