Mill House, Hollowmere
3 March 1854

To the Secretary, the Wessex Mutual Assurance Society

Sir,

I write in place of my father, Josiah Reeve, miller of this parish, who was buried on the seventeenth of February last. The policy on the mill at Hollowmere stood in his name and I am his executor.

I enclose the inventory of what was lost in the fire of the fourth of October last, drawn up by Thomas Wick, parish clerk, and valued by Matthew Carrow, blacksmith, and myself. The whole comes to ninety-six pounds. I ask that the Society settle the claim at its earliest convenience, as I intend to rebuild and cannot engage masons without the money in hand.

I remain, Sir, your obedient servant,
Samuel Reeve
