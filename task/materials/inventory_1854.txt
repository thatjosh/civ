INVENTORY OF THE CONTENTS OF HOLLOWMERE MILL LOST BY FIRE ON 4 OCTOBER 1853.
Drawn up by Thomas Wick, parish clerk, 20 February 1854. Valued by Matthew Carrow, blacksmith, and Samuel Reeve.
Sums in pounds.

Item                                   Pounds
Pair of French burr stones                 40
Water wheel, oak with iron shrouds         25
Sack hoist and tackle                       6
Dressing machine and cloths                12
Flour bins and sacks                        5
Weights, scales and sundries                8

The valuers have not carried a total; the Society's clerk will add it.
