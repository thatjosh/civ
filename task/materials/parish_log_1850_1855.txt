HOLLOWMERE PARISH LOG, 1850 TO 1855.
Kept by Thomas Wick, parish clerk. One entry per line, dated year-month-day.

1850-03-03  Vestry agreed the repair of the church roof; cost 14 pounds.
1850-11-19  Eliza Bramwell appointed schoolmistress.
1851-03-30  Census taken by the clerk. Twelve households counted.
1851-07-08  Baptism: Ann, daughter of Robert and Susan Hale.
1852-04-21  Daniel Grice licensed to keep the Plough a further year.
1852-10-02  Burial: Robert Fenwick of Netherby, brother to Alice Fenwick, aged 70.
1853-10-04  The mill burned in the night. No lives lost. Josiah Reeve, miller, saved the ledgers.
1853-10-06  Collection for the Reeve family: 3 pounds 11 shillings.
1854-02-17  Burial: Josiah Reeve, miller, aged 50.
1854-03-10  The Wessex Mutual paid 80 pounds on the mill claim.
1854-06-01  Samuel Reeve engaged masons from Netherby to rebuild the mill.
1855-09-29  Michaelmas. The mill reopened. Samuel Reeve, miller.
1855-12-24  Christmas collection for the poor: 2 pounds 4 shillings.
